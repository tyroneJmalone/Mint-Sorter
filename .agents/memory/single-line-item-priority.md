---
name: Single-line-item priority
description: Agreed business meaning and scope of the optional single-line-first sorting modifier.
---

Single-line-item priority is optional and off by default. An order with one product row and quantity five is a single-line order; a totals-only continuation is not another product row. Count the whole order, not just its first page.

**Why:** The user wants single-line packing slips first, sorted by their chosen method, followed by multi-line slips sorted by that same method.

**How to apply:** Preserve complete orders and stable selected-method ordering within both groups. Spreadsheet sequencing stays authoritative within each group, and labels follow the final slip sequence. Apply priority separately within each birth-year batch. Exclude unrelated presets and matching labels to an unchanged existing slip order. Do not guess when the row count cannot be established reliably.
